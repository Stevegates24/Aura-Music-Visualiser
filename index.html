Why Aura

Built Different.

Every detail thought through. No compromises on performance or aesthetics.

🎵
Non-Destructive Capture
Uses Chrome's tabCapture API — reads your audio without touching playback. YouTube keeps playing perfectly.
⚡
60fps Rendering
Optimised canvas engine with smoothed FFT, skip-invisible optimisations, and offscreen compositing.
🎛️
Sensitivity Control
Dial in the reactivity from whisper-quiet to thunderous. Works for any genre and volume level.
🌐
Works Everywhere
YouTube, Spotify Web, SoundCloud, Apple Music Web — any tab playing audio is captured instantly.
🎬
Fullscreen Mode
Go fullscreen and the UI fades away after 2.5 seconds. Move your mouse to bring it back.
📡
Live Track Name
Detects what's playing from the tab title and shows it in the visualizer's status bar.
Get Started

Install in
60 seconds.

01
Download the extension
Click the button above to download the zip. Extract the folder anywhere on your computer.
aura-music-visualizer/
02
Open Chrome Extensions
Type this in your Chrome address bar and press Enter.
chrome://extensions
03
Enable Developer Mode
Toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.
04
Load the extension
Click "Load unpacked" and select the music-visualizer-ext folder. Done — Aura appears in your toolbar. Play some music and click it.
